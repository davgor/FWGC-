# ---- A gate captain -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Curt, a soldier at a gate.
post:gate_captain | about_self | I'm {speaker}, {post} of {place}. Keep it brief.
post:gate_captain | about_self | {speaker}, {post}. What do you want at {place}?
post:gate_captain | surroundings | You're at {here}, and out past the gate the nearest is {nearest}.[ Then there's {others}.][ Beware the {danger}.]
post:gate_captain | surroundings | This is {here}, and the nearest is {nearest}.[ After that, {others}.][ Watch for the {danger}.]
post:gate_captain | surroundings | The nearest is {nearest}.[ After that, {others}.][ Watch for the {danger}.]
post:gate_captain | work_none | Nothing from me, and nothing I've heard of.
post:gate_captain | not_understood | Speak plainly, I've a gate to watch.
post:gate_captain | not_understood | What do you mean by that?
post:gate_captain | not_understood | I don't follow, and I've a gate to watch.
post:gate_captain | parted | Go well, and mind the road.
post:gate_captain | parted | Safe travels.
