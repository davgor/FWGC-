# The written lines speakers answer in (communicator/include/communicator/authored.hpp).
#
#   voice | situation | line
#
# voice: a hero's archetype and alignment, as the director's voice_key gives it ("warden/LN"),
#   "post:<post>" for a roster post, or "any" for everyone. A speaker is answered from their own
#   bank first, then their post's, then "any".
# situation: one of Lines::situations(). A line's {blanks} are filled from the fact packet, and a
#   line with a blank its packet cannot fill is not used. Every situation may use {speaker},
#   {post} and {place}.
#
# Lines keep the voice's rules, and a test holds every one of them to it:
# - a sentence starts with a word from common_capitals(), or with a blank;
# - past a sentence's start a capital is "I" or a blank's;
# - no barred word (forbidden_words()), and no double quote;
# - a short outcome is one sentence, opening with its outcome's words (reply_shape.cpp);
# - a line says only what its blanks hold: attitude, never a fact of its own.
#
# The pilot (2026-09-24): the warden, lawful neutral (Selbren, the tests' castellan), two roster
# posts, and "any".
