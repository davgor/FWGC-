# A session to listen to: orchestrator_session plays it against a world, and
# communicator_eval --session speaks it. The orchestrator's tools/session.cpp says how a script
# reads. It is a conversation a player might have: random townsfolk met more than once, work
# taken and done, and each kind of hidden face.
world 99 33 30
who armorer townsfolk 0
who local townsfolk 7
who villager townsfolk 20
who giver giver 0
who mask mask 0
who beast beast 0
who heart heart 0

# A first meeting with someone in town.
say armorer greet | Hello there!
say armorer small_talk | How's the weather treating you?
say armorer ask_surroundings | What's around here?
say armorer ask_safety | Is it safe to walk about?
say armorer ask_about_place @place | Tell me more about that place.
say armorer ask_surroundings | Sorry, what's around here again?
say armorer thank | Thanks, that helps.
say armorer farewell | Goodbye for now.

# Someone else, then back to the first.
say local greet | Good day to you.
say local small_talk | Quiet day, isn't it?
say local ask_for_work | Anyone hiring around here?
say local farewell | Take care.
say armorer greet | Me again.
say armorer ask_safety | Is it still safe, do you think?
say armorer farewell | Bye.

advance 4
say armorer greet | Hello again!
say armorer small_talk | Still cold out?
say armorer insult | You were no help at all last time.
say armorer greet | Well?
say armorer farewell | Bye.

advance 30
say armorer greet | It's been a while.
say armorer thank | Sorry for what I said before. Thank you for your help.
say armorer farewell | Farewell.

# A villager met twice, weeks apart.
say villager greet | Hail.
say villager ask_surroundings | Where am I, exactly?
say villager farewell | Farewell.
advance 20
say villager greet | Remember me?
say villager small_talk | How are things with you?
say villager farewell | Good day.

# A giver: work taken, finished, and remembered.
say giver greet | Good day.
say giver ask_for_work | Any work for me?
say giver accept_quest @own | I'll do it.
say giver farewell | I'll be back when it's done.
advance 6
resolve giver finished
say giver greet | It's done.
say giver thank | Thanks for the chance.
say giver farewell | Farewell.

# A mask: denied twice, exposed at the third meeting, then its true face.
say mask greet | Hello.
say mask confront | You're not what you seem.
say mask farewell | Farewell.
advance 1
say mask greet | Hello again.
say mask confront | I still think you're hiding something.
say mask farewell | Farewell.
advance 1
say mask greet | Me again.
say mask confront | Drop the act. I know what you are.
say mask small_talk | So, how are things really?
say mask farewell | Farewell.

# A beast: calm, unleashed under the full moon, then owning to it.
calmday

say beast farewell | Goodbye.
fullmoon
say beast greet | Hello? Are you all right?
say beast farewell | I'll go.
calmday
say beast greet | Hello again.

say beast farewell | Take care.

# A fractured heart, met on two days running, then confronted.
say heart greet | Hello.
say heart farewell | Goodbye.
advance 1
say heart greet | Hello again.
