# A regular: one townsperson met again and again over six weeks, as a player comes back to
# someone they like, and a second met in passing. What a conversation needs past the first
# meeting: greetings that remember, small talk that answers what was asked, no line said the same
# way twice running, a quarrel and its mending. orchestrator_session plays it (tools/session.cpp
# says how a script reads); communicator_eval --session speaks it.
world 99 33 30
who baker townsfolk 20
who smith townsfolk 3

# First meeting.
say baker greet | Morning!
say baker small_talk | How's the baking going?
say baker ask_surroundings | What's around here?
say baker ask_about_place @place | What's this town like, then?
say baker farewell | See you soon.

# The next day.
advance 1
say baker greet | Hello again.
say baker small_talk | Bit chilly this morning, isn't it?
say baker ask_safety | Anything I should watch out for around here?
say baker thank | Thanks, that's good to know.
say baker farewell | Take care.

# A few days on.
advance 3
say baker greet | Me again.
say baker small_talk | You look tired.
say baker small_talk | Think the weather will hold?
say baker farewell | Bye for now.

# A week on: a quarrel.
advance 7
say baker greet | Hello!
say baker small_talk | Busy day?
say baker insult | Your bread was stale last time.
say baker small_talk | Nice weather, anyway.
say baker farewell | Fine, I'm going.

# Two days on: making it up.
advance 2
say baker greet | Hello... about the other day.
say baker apologize | I'm sorry. Thank you for putting up with me.
say baker small_talk | How have you been?
say baker farewell | Goodbye.

# Three weeks on.
advance 20
say baker greet | It's been a while!
say baker small_talk | Lovely day, isn't it?
say baker ask_safety | Is it still safe around here?
say baker farewell | Farewell, friend.

# Someone else, met in passing the same day.
say smith greet | Good day.
say smith small_talk | Hard at work, I see.
say smith small_talk | Hot work in this weather?
say smith farewell | Goodbye.
