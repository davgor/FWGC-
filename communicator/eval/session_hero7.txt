# The golden benchmark's seventh session, held out: the stranger's name (contract version 24). Written
# 2026-09-29, before it was ever run. Another world, its first hero who gives work, and someone who stands
# where the hero does. The stranger gives a name when asked, is heard of by it, and later gives the hero
# another. Each line is labelled with the act it means.
world 5 33 30
who hero giver 0
who local near hero 0

# The game calls on heroes to act as days pass (contract version 26): today, each day of a short gap,
# each week of a long one; word of what they did goes round before each return.
heroes

# A first meeting: the hero asks who the stranger is.
say hero greet | Good evening.
say hero introduce =Maren | Maren. And you?
say hero small_talk | Busy night?
say hero ask_for_work | I could use some work, if you have any.
say hero accept_quest @own | I'll take it.
say hero farewell | Good night, then.

# Out of sight: compacted; the work is done three days on, and word of it goes round.

advance 1
