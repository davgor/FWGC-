# The golden benchmark's sixth session, held out: written 2026-09-28, after every earlier session had
# been fixed against (so none is held out any more) and before this one was ever run. Another world,
# the first hero there who gives work, other words. Judged blind as the first (communicator/eval/README.md,
# "The hero benchmark"). Each line is labelled with the act it means.
world 64 33 30
who hero giver 0

# The game calls on heroes to act as days pass (contract version 26): today, each day of a short gap,
# each week of a long one; word of what they did goes round before each return.
heroes

# A first meeting.
say hero greet | Evening. Are you the one they send people to?
say hero ask_about_them | What should I call you?
say hero small_talk | Long day?
say hero ask_for_work | I'm between jobs. Got anything for me?
say hero ask_about_them | Why can't anyone here do it?
say hero answer | I served two winters as a caravan guard.
say hero accept_quest @own | All right, count me in.
say hero ask_about_them | What happens to you if this fails?
say hero farewell | I'll be off, then.

# Out of sight: compacted; the work is done three days on.

advance 1
