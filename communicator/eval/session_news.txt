# News (contract version 11): what people hear of what happened, and of what the player did.
# Before anything happens, a neighbour has heard nothing. The player finishes a giver's work, the
# game's cron job shares the news, and the giver's neighbours have heard: they greet the player as
# the one who did it, and bring it up. Someone standing far off has not heard. Then a scare where
# the neighbours stand: the player talks one down, the game reports the threat beaten by the player,
# news goes round again, and a neighbour says so in small talk. orchestrator_session plays it
# (tools/session.cpp says how a script reads); communicator_eval --session speaks it.
world 99 33 30
who giver giver 0
who neighbour near giver 0
who other near giver 1
who far townsfolk 40

# Before anything happens: nothing heard.
say neighbour greet | Hello there.
say neighbour small_talk | Quiet day?
say neighbour farewell | Bye.

# The work, taken; the stranger goes to do it, and comes back a day on with it done.
say giver greet | Good day.
say giver ask_for_work | Any work going?
say giver accept_quest @own | I'll do it.
say giver farewell | I'll be back when it's done.
advance 1
resolve giver finished
share
say giver greet | It's done.
say giver farewell | Farewell.

# The giver's neighbours have heard. One greets the stranger with it, then is asked for news; the
# other tells it in small talk.
say neighbour greet | Hello again.
say neighbour small_talk | Any news?
say neighbour farewell | Take care.
say other greet | Good day to you.
say other ask_surroundings | What's around here?
say other ask_about_place @place | Tell me about this place.
say other small_talk | Heard anything lately?
say other farewell | Bye.

# Someone standing far off has not heard of it: asked for news, they give their own place's.
say far greet | Hello.
say far small_talk | Any news?
say far small_talk | Nice weather.
say far farewell | Bye.

# A scare where the neighbours stand, talked down, and beaten off by the player.
advance 0.25
fright neighbour 2
minutes 20
say neighbour calm | It's all right. You're safe.
report neighbour beaten player
share
say other small_talk | Quite a day!
say other farewell | Look after yourself.
