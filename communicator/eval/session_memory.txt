# Short and long memory (contract version 12): a speaker remembers the stranger across compactions.
# The stranger meets a townsperson, tells them something of themselves, asks what is around and about
# a place, and goes. The game compacts the townsperson (out of sight, their log made into long
# memory). Days on, the townsperson greets the stranger with what the stranger told them, and asked
# of the place again, says they told of it before. Then the owner's example: a villager who has heard
# only that work was asked for in their town overhears its giver greet the stranger once the work is
# done, is compacted, and greets the stranger as the one who did it.
# orchestrator_session plays it (tools/session.cpp says how a script reads); communicator_eval
# --session speaks it.
world 99 33 30
who smith townsfolk 0
who giver giver 0
who villager near giver 0

# A first meeting: something of the stranger, and a place asked about.
say smith greet | Good morning.
say smith small_talk | Busy day?
say smith answer | I'm on my way to see my sister. She keeps the mill at the ford.
say smith ask_surroundings | What's around here?
say smith ask_about_place @place | Tell me about that place.
say smith farewell | Thanks. Goodbye.

# Out of sight: the game compacts the smith, and days pass.
compact smith
advance 3
say smith greet | Hello again!
say smith ask_about_place @place | Remind me about that place?
say smith small_talk | How have you been?
say smith farewell | Take care.

# The owner's example. The villager has heard the work was asked for.
share villager
say giver greet | Good day.
say giver ask_for_work | Any work going?
say giver accept_quest @own | I'll do it.
say giver farewell | I'll be back when it's done.
advance 1
resolve giver finished
say giver greet | It's done.
overhear villager
say giver farewell | Farewell.

# Out of sight: the game compacts the villager, and days pass. No word has gone round since.
compact villager
advance 2
say villager greet | Hello there.
say villager small_talk | Any news?
say villager farewell | Bye.
