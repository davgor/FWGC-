# A panic: something frightens a townsperson, her fear spreads to two neighbours, and the player
# talks them down one at a time, while one the player insults will not be eased until the insult
# is mended. What feelings need (contract version 9): frightened words to go with frightened
# faces, fear that spreads and falls, and calming that works a step at a time and not while the
# danger lasts. orchestrator_session plays it (tools/session.cpp says how a script reads);
# communicator_eval --session speaks it.
world 99 33 30
who miller townsfolk 5
who weaver townsfolk 6
who carter townsfolk 7

# Something frightens the miller, and her neighbours see her panic.
fright miller 3
gather miller weaver carter
say miller greet | Hello? Are you all right?
say miller calm | It's all right, you're safe now.
say miller ask_surroundings | What's around here?

# A quarter of an hour on, whatever frightened her has passed.
minutes 16
say miller calm | Easy. It's passed. Breathe.
say miller calm | You're doing well. Slow breaths.
say miller small_talk | Is the mill busy these days?
say miller thank | Thank you for talking with me.
say miller farewell | Take care of yourself.

# The weaver, afraid from the miller's panic.
say weaver greet | Hello there.
say weaver calm | Don't be afraid. It's over.
say weaver ask_safety | Is it safe around here, usually?
say weaver farewell | Goodbye.

# The carter, whom the player insults while he is frightened.
say carter greet | You there.
say carter insult | Pull yourself together, you coward.
say carter calm | Easy, easy. I didn't mean it.
say carter apologize | I'm sorry. That was cruel of me.
say carter calm | It's all right now.
say carter farewell | Farewell.

# An hour later, the miller again.
minutes 60
say miller greet | Hello again. Feeling better?
say miller calm | You're safe, you know.
say miller farewell | Bye.
