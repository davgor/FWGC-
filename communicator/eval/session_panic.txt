# A panic: something frightens a townsperson, the fear spreads to two others, and the player
# talks them down one at a time, while one the player insults will not be eased until the insult
# is mended. What feelings need (contract version 9): frightened words to go with frightened
# faces, fear that spreads and falls, and calming that works a step at a time and not while the
# danger lasts. orchestrator_session plays it (tools/session.cpp says how a script reads);
# communicator_eval --session speaks it.
world 99 33 30
who first townsfolk 5
who second townsfolk 6
who third townsfolk 7

# Something frightens the first townsperson, and two others see the panic. (The picker takes
# townsfolk one to a place, so they stand apart; the game would report them together.)
fright first 3
gather first second third
say first greet | Hello? Are you all right?
say first calm | It's all right, you're safe now.
say first ask_surroundings | What's around here?

# A quarter of an hour on, whatever frightened them has passed.
minutes 16
say first calm | Easy. It's passed. Breathe.
say first calm | You're doing well. Slow breaths.
say first small_talk | Busy day, then?
say first thank | Thank you for talking with me.
say first farewell | Take care of yourself.

# The second, afraid from the first's panic.
say second greet | Hello there.
say second calm | Don't be afraid. It's over.
say second ask_safety | Is it safe around here, usually?
say second farewell | Goodbye.

# The third, whom the player insults while they are frightened.
say third greet | You there.
say third insult | Pull yourself together, you coward.
say third calm | Easy, easy. I didn't mean it.
say third apologize | I'm sorry. That was cruel of me.
say third calm | It's all right now.
say third farewell | Farewell.

# An hour later, the first again.
minutes 60
say first greet | Hello again. Feeling better?
say first calm | You're safe, you know.
say first farewell | Bye.
