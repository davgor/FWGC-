# The golden benchmark's third session, held out: written 2026-09-28, after the fixes drawn from the
# second session (which is no longer held out), and before this one was ever run. Another hero,
# another world, other words. Judged blind as the first (communicator/eval/README.md, "The hero
# benchmark"). Each line is labelled with the act it means.
world 7 33 30
who hero giver 2

# A first meeting.
say hero greet | Morning. Is this your post?
say hero ask_about_them | What's your name?
say hero small_talk | Busy day?
say hero ask_about_them | What are you trying to do out here?
say hero ask_for_work | I could use the work. Anything going?
say hero ask_about_them | Why can't you handle it yourself?
say hero answer | I've never been much of a fighter, but I'm quick.
say hero accept_quest @own | Fine, I'm in.
say hero thank | I appreciate the trust.
say hero farewell | See you soon.

# Out of sight: compacted; the work is done four days on.
compact hero
advance 4
resolve hero finished
say hero greet | Hello again. It's done.
say hero small_talk | Was it worth all that?
say hero answer | We lost two good people out there.
say hero complain | You never said there'd be so many of them.
say hero apologize | Sorry. It's been a long few days.
say hero farewell | I'll be at the inn.

# A month later.
compact hero
advance 30
say hero greet | It's been a while.
say hero small_talk | Anything changed since?
say hero ask_about_them | Who are your enemies?
say hero ask_about_them | Do you ever miss home?
say hero farewell | Stay safe.
