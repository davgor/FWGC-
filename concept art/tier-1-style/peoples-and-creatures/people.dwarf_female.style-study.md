A thick reinforced leather smith's apron over burgundy rolled sleeves, dark trousers and stout leather work boots establishes her working craft role. Copper fittings, restrained geometric stitching, small steel reinforcements, tool belt, forging hammer, tongs, punch and work glove provide practical detail. Carry forward substantial materials without reverting to a guard's breastplate, pauldrons, mail suit or full leg armor.

This is a distinct cultural reference alongside the armored male: different face, hairstyle, clothing silhouette, palette, equipment, pose and occupation. Neither outfit defines all members of its sex. The owner explicitly removed the full beard; this approved clean-shaven variant overrides the initial catalogue-derived beard choice. Generator data has not been changed; any catalogue reconciliation remains separate scoped work.

One full standing figure and one matching headshot, in the approved restrained anime-painterly style. Proportions and tools remain illustrative rather than production specifications. The approval does not complete the full art bible or production checklists.
