## Approved direction

A complete inhabited riverside village in an elevated three-quarter landscape view. Timber-framed cream plaster cottages, stone foundations, slate roofs and occasional thatch surround a green with a well and mature tree. A stone bridge connects the approach road to the village; a riverside watermill, inn and open-front forge provide useful focal buildings. Orchards, kitchen gardens, fenced grazing and cultivated fields connect the village to its surrounding rolling countryside.

Preserve the restrained anime-painterly rendering, readable architectural silhouettes, natural green landscape, warm timber and cream walls, slate roofs and small red/blue textile accents. Buildings should share practical construction while varying in footprint, roof shape and function.
